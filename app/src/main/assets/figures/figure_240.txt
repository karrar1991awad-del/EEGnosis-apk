Pyruvate dehydrogenase deﬁ  ciency (PDH), a deﬁ  ciency in the E1 (pyruvate dehydrogenase) component of the pyruvate dehydrogenase complex, is one of the most common 
genetic defects of mitochondrial energy metabolism. PDH deﬁ  ciency is an extremely heterogeneous condition, both in clinical presentation and in the severity of the biochemical 
abnormality, with a clinical spectrum ranging from fatal lactic acidosis in the newborn period to a chronic neurodegenerative condition with gross structural abnormalities in the 
central nervous system.
