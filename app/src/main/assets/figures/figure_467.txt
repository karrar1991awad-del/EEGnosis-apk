was associated with favorable outcome in 67%. The nonreactive rhythmic pattern was associated with unfavorable outcome in 60%. Sequential EEG recordings are required to 
detect the evolution of rhythmic coma patterns during childhood.55 The prognosis depends mainly on the primary disease process rather than the EEG ﬁ  nding. The prognosis
