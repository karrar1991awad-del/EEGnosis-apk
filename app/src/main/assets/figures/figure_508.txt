Although PFA is very common in the EEG of Lennox-Gastaut syndrome, it can also be seen in infantile spasms,15 progressive partial epilepsy, and atypical generalized epilepsy.79 
The minimal substrate for the production of seizures consists of spike-wave/polyspike-wave complexes and runs of fast activity in the neocortex.80
