Midline spikes occur almost exclusively in children, are strongly associated with clinical seizures, and are activated by sleep.80,200,201 Cz is the most frequent spike location. Most 
patients without sleep activation had CNS disease. Adults with midline spikes may represent a distinct entity with a worse prognosis. Midline spikes showed strong correlations 
with clinical seizures; 91% had epileptic seizures of diverse types. Over two-thirds of the patients were neurologically impaired. Midline spikes most probably originate from 
discharging lesions of the mesial or paramedian region of the cerebral cortex.80,202,203 Epilepsy with midline spikes is not necessarily benign.203
