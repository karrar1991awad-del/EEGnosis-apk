Focal transmantle dysplasia is a malformation of cortical development that extends through the entire cerebral mantle, from the ventricular surface to the cerebral cortex. 
The presence of balloon cells suggests that these malformations are associated with maldiﬀ erentiation of the stem cells generated in the germinal zone.214
PLEDs usually represent acute or subacute cerebral insults. However, in a review of 96 patients with PLEDs, 9 cases of chronic PLEDs (6.2%) were seen in patients with 
symptomatic focal epilepsy caused by underlying focal cortical dysplasia or severe remote cerebral injury.215
