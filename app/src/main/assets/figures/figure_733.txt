FIGURE 9 125. Simple Partial Seizures; Focal Encephalomalacia Due to Viral Meningoencephalitis in Newborn. A 15-year-old boy with medically intractable epilepsy 
and severe left hemiparesis due to viral meningoencephalitis during the neonatal period. Almost all seizures were characterized by sensory or versive seizures with preserved 
consciousness.
Coronal and axial T2-weighted and sagittal T1-weighted images show right cerebral hemiatrophy with abnormal intensity in the right insular, frontal, and parietal regions. 
Interictal EEG shows very frequent sharp waves in the right frontal-anterior temporal regions.
