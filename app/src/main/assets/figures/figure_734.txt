In patients with seizures lasting >30 minutes, only 23% of the survivors were normal at follow-up; 34% showed developmental deterioration and 36% developed new-
onset epilepsy.230 Mortality is related to etiology and is higher in younger children and with multifocal or generalized abnormalities on the initial EEG. RSE due to either an 
acute symptomatic etiology or a progressive encephalopathy was associated with the highest mortality. Only one survivor did not have active epilepsy. A shorter duration of 
suppressive therapy, ultimately with the same outcome and possibly fewer complications, was recommended.231
