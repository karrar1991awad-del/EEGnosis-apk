FIGURE 9 133. Herpes Simplex Encephalitis; Simple Partial Seizure. A 2½-year-old boy with fever, intermittent episodes of right facial twitching, drooling, and dysarthria 
without altered mental status. CSF examination showed mild lymphocytosis with normal protein and glucose. PCR for HSV type 1 was positive. Cranial MRI shows bilateral rolandic 
involvement, left greater than right (arrow). EEG demonstrates semi-rhythmic sharply-contoured theta activity and sharp waves in the left midtemporal region.
