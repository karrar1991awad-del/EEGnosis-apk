HSE must be considered in all children who present with acute onset of focal seizures, especially when associated with febrile illnesses. Acute onset of weakness of masseter, 
facial, pharyngeal, and glossal muscles, accompanied by fever, headache, and focal motor seizures of the face, should suggest HSE involving the bilateral frontal and parietal
